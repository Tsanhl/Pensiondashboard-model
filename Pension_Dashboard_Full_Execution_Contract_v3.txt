00. NORTH STAR: RELIABLE ANSWERS, NOT ACTIVITY COUNTS

The outcome is an ordinary user receiving a useful, supported pension answer, based on the right user's records and applicable evidence, with necessary qualifications and working citations, delivered reliably in the actual browser. Training and ingestion are interventions; evaluation measures failures and gains. None is the outcome by itself.

Apply these six principles as executable acceptance requirements:

P1 — Define reliability. Wrong-user data, unsupported legal scope, invented certainty and unavailable answers hidden behind a spinner fail. A generic referral fails an answerable question. A supported partial explanation plus a necessary clarification can be correct when a specific conclusion is genuinely underdetermined; preserve the unresolved scope. Availability failures, safe refusals, appropriate clarification and completed substantive answers must have different outcomes and denominators.

P2 — Fix the first failed boundary. Evaluate -> locate the cause -> make a relevant intervention -> reevaluate. Missing authority calls for controlled evidence work; dropped evidence for retrieval/context repair; wrong record/number for data/calculation repair; a distorted raw answer for generation analysis and possibly training; a correct raw answer damaged downstream for validator/rendering repair. Do not use repeated training as a universal repair.

P3 — Separate feasibility from effectiveness. A two-update resource proof establishes only bounded trainability. Completing the authorised update budget establishes only completion of that declared experiment; 48 is a maximum comparator in the initial duration study, not an assumed optimum. Paired answer evaluation establishes only observed behavioural improvement. Browser and formal acceptance establish their respective scoped results. Do not require all five legal failures to pass before training whose purpose is to repair them. Do require their acceptance before candidate promotion/qualification.

P4 — Demonstrate generalisation. Test non-identical wording, changed evidence, multiple schemes, prior-turn facts, zero versus missing, stale/conflicting records and correct no-clarification cases. Do not hardcode the five questions. Training, validation, development comparisons and sealed testing have different roles; report overlap honestly.

P5 — Evaluate the evaluators. A PASS must identify what was checked, against which original evidence, by which exact reviewer/configuration, on which exact answer. Citation presence is not entailment; low loss is not legal competence. Two isolated reviewers may share errors. Use calibrated positive/negative controls, inspect material disagreements and preserve failed attempts. Do not relax hard requirements to raise throughput.

P6 — Preserve independence and maintain the product. Sealed unseen is not for selecting adapters, repairing prompts or finding new sources during the exam. Freeze the actual candidate before its separately authorised use. Subsequent model/code/prompt/corpus changes require new versioned evidence and impact testing; old qualification does not silently carry over. An AI review is not professional legal certification.
