# Evidence Priority and Conflict Rules

1. Current applicable legislation and statutory instruments.
2. Binding case law, ordered by court hierarchy and considering whether the point is ratio, obiter, distinguished or overruled.
3. Current official guidance from the Pensions Regulator, FCA, HMRC, DWP, the Pensions Ombudsman, MoneyHelper or another competent authority.
4. Verified scheme trust deed, rules, policy or member communication for scheme-specific terms, subject to overriding law.
5. Peer-reviewed or practitioner journals and licensed commentary as secondary interpretation only.
6. Reviewed study notes as navigation aids only; never as sole evidence for a user-facing legal proposition.

When sources disagree, preserve both relevant views in retrieval. The answer must identify the hierarchy, decision date, jurisdiction, commencement/version and factual distinction. A higher-ranked source does not make an irrelevant proposition relevant, and a later source does not automatically overrule a binding earlier authority.

Original enacted PDFs are archival primary materials. The answer corpus must use the latest available revised legislation snapshot and separately check commencement and outstanding amendments before stating that a provision is in force.
